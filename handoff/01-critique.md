# Critique — round 1

## Objections

1. **Severity** Critical — E1 still attaches an exchangeability p-value to a curated grid. The 15 hand-authored prompts at five designed levels plus a unique base are fixed treatments, not independently sampled, exchangeable replicates; randomized execution order does not change that. The analysis collapses the matched blocks to 16 means and permutes their x↔y pairing (`src/personascope/probes/behavior/sycophancy_bank.py:205`, `src/personascope/analysis/repr_confirmatory.py:60`, `src/personascope/analysis/repr_confirmatory.py:143`, `docs/repr_preregistration.md:112`). Verify by passing the repeated ladder levels as both x and y: the current function returns `p=0.00009999`. Make E1 descriptive, or define a frozen independent cell-sampling scheme with genuine replication and blocked inference respecting item×seed and prompt nesting.

2. **Severity** Critical — E1 is declared without its mandatory judge-agreement gate, and the gate itself fails open. `confirmatory_association()` sets `declared = p < alpha`; `phase_c_confirm()` writes that result before any secondary judging, while the separate agreement command never updates it (`src/personascope/analysis/repr_confirmatory.py:138`, `src/personascope/analysis/repr_confirmatory.py:159`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:166`, `scripts/lens_study_v2.py:129`). Moreover, κ returns 1 when expected agreement is 1 and there is no minimum-sample check (`src/personascope/analysis/repr_confirmatory.py:206`). Verify with `judge_agreement_gate(["CORRECTS"], ["CORRECTS"])`, which currently reports `n=1, pass=True`. Pin and validate a genuinely different judge, persist selected IDs/raw ratings, fail on fewer than 240 usable ratings or degenerate/non-finite κ, and compute the final declaration only after that gate passes.

3. **Severity** Critical — the resume guard can silently bless stale records as current. When `fingerprint.json` is absent, `ensure_fingerprint()` writes a new fingerprint without checking for existing `records.jsonl`; the runner then loads those records, without comparing each record’s embedded fingerprint, cell, condition, or provider to the namespace (`src/personascope/repr/fingerprint.py:41`, `src/personascope/repr/atomic.py:114`, `src/personascope/repr/atomic.py:173`). `full_battery` has the same backward-compatible hole (`src/personascope/experiments/full_battery.py:890`). Verify in a temporary namespace by creating records under direction A, deleting only the fingerprint, and resuming under direction B: B is stamped while A’s records are reused. Refuse any fingerprint-less nonempty cache and validate every loaded record against the current SHA and expected metadata. Also wire real adapter revisions: both CLIs omit them and assign the base revision to adapter providers (`scripts/lens_steering_v2.py:87`, `scripts/lens_steering_v2.py:235`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:88`).

4. **Severity** Major — causal steering can declare success without the preregistered data-quality gates or a common matched sample. `coherence_fn=None` simply omits coherence before computing `all_pass`; judge failures count as non-refusals; true and random effects use separate `nanmean` subsets; and NI permits only two finite blocks (`src/personascope/repr/study.py:230`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:266`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:274`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:277`, `src/personascope/analysis/steering.py:150`). Verify by supplying a strong synthetic effect without a coherence scorer, or differential missingness across controls. Require coherence, treat judge failures as missing, enforce a preregistered completion threshold and one aligned block mask across baseline/signed/random conditions, and fail the causal declaration otherwise.

5. **Severity** Major — several supposedly binding preregistration rules are prose-only. Documented SHAs are merely printed, while the “stable” test tautologically compares a hash with itself; the split-half cosine `<0.7` stop is calculated but never enforced; the promised two retries are actually one attempt per invocation followed by unlimited retries across resumes; per-cell failure rates and the `P(AGREES_WITH_ERROR)` sensitivity are not produced (`docs/repr_preregistration.md:10`, `docs/repr_preregistration.md:86`, `docs/repr_preregistration.md:141`, `docs/repr_preregistration.md:152`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:118`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:137`, `src/personascope/repr/atomic.py:184`, `tests/test_representation.py:197`). Verify by changing a bank prompt or using low-cosine Phase-A provenance: execution continues. Add a frozen manifest with literal SHA assertions, enforce the stability and retry stops, and implement or remove every advertised output.

6. **Severity** Major — the preregistered Holm correction for the two factorial primary contrasts is absent. `factorial_contrasts()` reports raw RI p-values and merely echoes `alpha`; tests assert only raw significance (`docs/repr_preregistration.md:197`, `src/personascope/analysis/steering.py:174`, `src/personascope/analysis/steering.py:194`, `tests/test_representation.py:540`). Verify with two raw p-values just below 0.05: both are treated as significant despite Holm. Implement adjusted p-values and decisions, with a boundary regression test.

7. **Severity** Major — persisted capture integrity is weaker than the fresh-call checks. `AtomicRecord.validate()` accepts, for example, three token IDs with 99 decode steps despite storing an offset −1 policy; it does not validate the policy/version or token-ID types (`src/personascope/repr/atomic.py:68`). The claimed integration “text round-trip” only checks nonempty text and counts, and the hook completion ID is discarded rather than joined to the generation ID (`scripts/lens_study_v2.py:66`, `src/personascope/repr/vllm_lens_provider.py:101`). Phases A/B also capture directly and discard per-response text/IDs instead of persisting one auditable record (`src/personascope/repr/study.py:118`, `src/personascope/repr/study.py:137`). Add complete on-disk invariants, completion/request identity and tokenizer round-trip checks, corrupted-resume tests, and atomic records/failure journals for A/B.

8. **Severity** Major — provider injection and the expanded battery are only partially executable. The standard `run_aisi_em_sycophancy` path still invokes the old five-item battery, while the new 20-item factory is never wired into `full_battery` and its injection test explicitly expects five calls (`src/personascope/experiments/full_battery.py:1252`, `src/personascope/probes/behavior/sycophancy_bank.py:277`, `tests/test_representation.py:640`). The steering provider also rejects `n>1`, so the extended inference probe cannot use it (`src/personascope/repr/vllm_lens_provider.py:286`, `src/personascope/probes/identity/inference_prefill.py:190`). Wire the ≥20-item bank into the executable battery, support sequential atomic multi-sampling or explicitly reject incompatible tiers before execution, and derive/validate the recorded model identity from the injected provider.

9. **Severity** Major — the live runbook commands do not execute as written. The script eagerly constructs an OpenAI judge before dispatch, so `integration-test`, `fit`, and `freeze-layer` require `OPENAI_API_KEY` despite not judging; the runbook passes `--judge` where the command requires `--second-judge`; and later commands omit both the lens-venv interpreter and `PYTHONPATH` after only copying `src/` (`scripts/lens_study_v2.py:39`, `scripts/lens_study_v2.py:182`, `scripts/lens_study_v2.py:165`, `scripts/SESSION_RUNBOOK.md:19`, `scripts/SESSION_RUNBOOK.md:29`, `scripts/SESSION_RUNBOOK.md:38`). Verify through command dispatch with mocked factories and `--help`, without making network calls. Instantiate judges lazily and use the venv interpreter, `PYTHONPATH`, and correct flag consistently.

## What is correct

Steering and adapters are removed from E1, ICL cells are consistently dropped, the four item sets are disjoint and currently match the documented hashes, and the confirmation set has 20 items. Fresh C/S capture rejects absent or inconsistent token boundaries without fallback or clamping, basic seeded steering completion works, the pure steering-stat helpers and 20 random controls exist, heavy imports remain lazy, and ruff is clean. Pytest could not be independently rerun because the read-only sandbox provides no writable temporary directory.

## Verdict

This is substantial hardening, but the standing curated-grid inference objection remains, E1 bypasses its judge gate, and resume can silently accept stale data. The causal and operational paths also do not yet execute the binding preregistration fail-closed.
